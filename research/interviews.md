# Practitioner interviews

This page is the log for interviews with people who do the roles in this
repository. It records which documents they rely on and which ones they are missing,
so templates are added for real gaps.

## How to record an interview

Add one row per interview to the log. Record a quote only when the person agreed to
be quoted, and never record a name, an employer, or anything that identifies them
without their written consent.

The columns are:

- **Role:** the person's role, using the role names in this repository.
- **Date:** the interview date, as `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Consent to quote:** Yes or No.
- **Duties:** the duties they described, in short phrases.
- **Documents they rely on:** the documents they use to do the work.
- **Missing documents:** the documents they need and do not have.
