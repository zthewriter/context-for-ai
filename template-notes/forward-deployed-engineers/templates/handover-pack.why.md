# Why the Handover pack template exists (FDE-04)

This note explains why the [Handover pack template](../../../forward-deployed-engineers/templates/handover-pack.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Engagements end, and the customer's team has to run what was built.

## What the classic document misses

A classic handover document lists deliverables. This template lists who owns each part, the open issues, and how to change the AI behavior safely.

## What goes wrong without it

The customer cannot change prompts, sources, or thresholds without calling you back.
