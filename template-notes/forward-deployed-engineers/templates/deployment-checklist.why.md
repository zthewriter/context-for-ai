# Why the Deployment checklist template exists (FDE-10)

This note explains why the [Deployment checklist template](../../../forward-deployed-engineers/templates/deployment-checklist.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Go-live in a customer environment involves steps on both sides, under time pressure.

## What the classic document misses

A go-live checklist lists steps. This template gives every step an owner and a sign-off, and names who decides to roll back.

## What goes wrong without it

A step falls between the two teams on go-live day.
