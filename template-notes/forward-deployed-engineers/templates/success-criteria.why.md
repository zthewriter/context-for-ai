# Why the Success criteria template exists (FDE-07)

This note explains why the [Success criteria template](../../../forward-deployed-engineers/templates/success-criteria.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Engagements end in disputes when done was never defined.

## What the classic document misses

Acceptance criteria in a statement of work are often feature lists. This template requires a measurable result with a baseline, a method, a period, and sign-off from both sides.

## What goes wrong without it

The system works, and the customer still says the engagement failed.
