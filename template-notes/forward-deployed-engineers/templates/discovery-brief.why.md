# Why the Discovery brief template exists (FDE-06)

This note explains why the [Discovery brief template](../../../forward-deployed-engineers/templates/discovery-brief.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Forward deployed engagements start with discovery, and what was learned usually lives in one engineer's notes.

## What the classic document misses

A requirements document lists what to build. This template records the problem in the customer's words, the systems and data involved, the decision makers, and the assumptions still to confirm.

## What goes wrong without it

The team builds against an assumption the customer never confirmed.
