# Why the Dataset card template exists (AIE-09)

This note explains why the [Dataset card template](../../../ai-engineers/templates/dataset-card.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Every quality number an AI team reports was measured on a dataset, and the number means nothing without it.

## What the classic document misses

A data dictionary describes fields. This template describes provenance, consent, splits, labeling, and known gaps, which decide whether a result generalizes.

## What goes wrong without it

A team reports a strong score on a dataset that does not cover the questions users actually ask.
