# Why the AEO strategy template exists (AEO-01)

This note explains why the [AEO strategy template](../../../templates/aeo/aeo-strategy.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

People increasingly ask an AI assistant before they search or visit a site, and the assistant answers in its own words. Teams need to decide which of those answers their content should shape, and what they allow answer engines to read.

## What the classic document misses

A classic SEO strategy targets a ranked list of links and measures position and clicks. An answer engine may cite a page, paraphrase it without a link, or skip it entirely, and an SEO strategy has no measure for any of those outcomes. It also rarely records an access policy for AI crawlers.

## What goes wrong without it

Content work is planned around rankings while the questions that matter are answered from someone else's page. Crawler access is decided ad hoc in robots.txt, and nobody can say whether a change helped.
