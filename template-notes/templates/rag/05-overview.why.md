# Why the Overview template exists (RAG-05)

This note explains why the [Overview template](../../../templates/rag/05-overview.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Evaluators, buyers, and new team members need to know what a RAG system is for before they read anything else.

## What the classic document misses

A classic product overview lists features. This template also states what the system will not do and who owns it, because an AI system's limits matter as much as its capabilities.

## What goes wrong without it

Readers assume the system can do more than it can and trust answers they should have checked.
