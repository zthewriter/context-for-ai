# Why the Confidence and actions template exists (DEC-08)

This note explains why the [Confidence and actions template](../../../templates/decision-model/08-confidence-and-actions.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Teams need a policy for what happens at each confidence level: act, ask a person, or refuse.

## What the classic document misses

No classic document covers this. A decision model returns a probability, and the policy that turns a probability into an action is the most consequential decision in the system.

## What goes wrong without it

Thresholds are set once by an engineer and never reviewed, and nobody outside the team knows when a person is asked to check.
