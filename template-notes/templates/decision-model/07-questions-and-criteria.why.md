# Why the Questions and criteria template exists (DEC-07)

This note explains why the [Questions and criteria template](../../../templates/decision-model/07-questions-and-criteria.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Decision models are configured with questions and criteria, and those are the system's real logic.

## What the classic document misses

A classic reference documents parameters. This template documents the wording of each question and criterion, because a change of one word changes the decisions.

## What goes wrong without it

Criteria are edited without review, and decisions change without anyone recording why.
