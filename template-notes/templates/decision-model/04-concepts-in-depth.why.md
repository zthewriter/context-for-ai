# Why the Decision model concepts in depth template exists (DEC-04)

This note explains why the [Decision model concepts in depth template](../../../templates/decision-model/04-concepts-in-depth.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Practitioners tuning a decision-model system need the mechanics behind each concept and the settings they can change.

## What the classic document misses

A classic deep dive explains how something works. This template ties each mechanism to the setting that controls it and the symptom a wrong value produces.

## What goes wrong without it

Teams change settings by trial and error and cannot explain why decisions got better or worse.
