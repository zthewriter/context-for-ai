# Why the Troubleshooting template exists (DEC-12)

This note explains why the [Troubleshooting template](../../../templates/decision-model/12-troubleshooting.md) exists. It lives outside the template folders, so copying a set does not copy it.

## What demand created this document

Support teams and readers of a decision-model system need fixes for the problems that actually occur.

## What the classic document misses

A classic troubleshooting guide covers errors. This template also covers wrong decisions that raise no error at all, because those are the most common problems an AI system has.

## What goes wrong without it

The most common complaint, a confident wrong decision, has no documented fix, so every report becomes a ticket.
